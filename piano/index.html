The piano problem

Reading or naming notes is only half the skill.

Pianists can understand theory and still hesitate when a note or chord tone has to be found immediately in a chosen range.

PitchStill keeps the work simple: one target, one response, immediate feedback, and session stats that show whether recall is getting cleaner.

  • Range-based practiceKeep drills inside the register you are trying to strengthen.
  • One target at a timeRemove clutter and build reliable note-to-key response.
  • Harmony in piecesPractice chord tones and scale patterns as notes you can actually land.

Piano FAQ

Common questions

Can I set a keyboard range?

Yes. Piano practice can stay inside a chosen note range so drills match your current goal.

Does this teach full piano technique?

No. It isolates note recall and pitch response. Use it alongside normal piano practice.

Is it tap-based theory practice?

No. You play the note and PitchStill listens through the microphone.

Can I practice harmony?

Yes. Premium expands into guided harmonic practice, sequence drills, inversions, and chord movement.
