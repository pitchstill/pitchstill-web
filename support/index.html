Support

Need help with PitchStill?

For account issues, purchase questions, bug reports, or general feedback, contact support@pitchstill.com.

Contact

Email: support@pitchstill.com

When reporting a bug, include your device model, iOS version, and what happened.

Common issues

  • Make sure microphone access is enabled for PitchStill in iOS Settings.
  • Keep the phone close enough to your instrument or voice during training.
  • For purchase restore issues, use the restore purchases option inside the app.

What PitchStill supports

  • Guitar
  • Bass
  • Piano
  • Vocals
