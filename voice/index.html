The vocal problem

Recognizing a note is different from producing it.

You may hear the target clearly and still need practice landing it with your voice. PitchStill keeps that skill focused and measurable.

The app plays or shows a target, listens while you sing, and confirms whether your pitch is close enough during the active session.

Voice FAQ

Common questions

Can I use this for singing in tune?

It can help you practice producing target notes, but it is not a replacement for vocal coaching or full singing technique.

Does the app record my voice?

No. Microphone input is used during active pitch detection. Audio is not recorded or stored.

Can I choose my vocal range?

Yes. PitchStill includes soprano, alto, tenor, and bass range options.

Is it traditional ear training?

The focus is active note recall and pitch production: hearing a target and singing it back.
