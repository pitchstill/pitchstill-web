Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Overview

PitchStill is a note training app for musicians. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Data We Collect

Microphone / audio

PitchStill accesses the microphone only during active training sessions so the app can detect the pitch you play or sing. Audio is processed in real time on your device. PitchStill does not record, store, or transmit audio.

Account data

If you create an account through Google Sign-In or email, PitchStill stores your email address and a unique user ID so cloud sync and authentication can work.

Training data

Session history, scores, and progress statistics are stored locally on your device. If you are signed in, this data may also sync to the cloud so it can be restored across devices.

Purchase status

PitchStill uses RevenueCat to verify premium entitlement status. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google, not by PitchStill directly.

Product analytics

PitchStill uses Mixpanel to understand app usage and improve the product. This may include events such as screen views, onboarding completion, session starts and completions, sequence-mode selection, paywall views, paywall button taps, and purchase completion. If you are signed in, these analytics events may be associated with your app account ID.

Advertising measurement

PitchStill uses Meta App Events SDK to measure app installs and advertising campaign performance. Meta may receive device identifiers, app launch or activation events, device information, and IP address for install attribution and ad measurement. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier is controlled by Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and your device privacy settings.

How We Use Data

  • To detect pitch during active note-training sessions
  • To authenticate optional user accounts
  • To sync session history and statistics across devices
  • To determine whether premium features should be unlocked
  • To measure onboarding, training, and upgrade flows so the app can be improved
  • To measure app installs and advertising campaign performance

PitchStill does not sell personal data. PitchStill shares limited data with service providers required to run the app and with Meta for advertising measurement.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase for authentication and cloud data storage
  • Google Sign-In for optional OAuth login
  • RevenueCat for purchase entitlement management
  • Mixpanel for product analytics
  • Meta App Events SDK for app install attribution and advertising measurement
  • Apple and Google for app distribution and payments

Retention and Deletion

If you use PitchStill without an account, your data remains on your device. If you use a signed-in account, cloud data remains available until you delete the account or contact support for removal assistance.

Children's Privacy

PitchStill is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pitchstill@gmail.com.
