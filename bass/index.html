The bass problem

Patterns are useful. The line needs targets.

Bass players need roots, thirds, fifths, sevenths, and connecting notes to be available quickly. If every change starts with a search, the groove has already moved on.

PitchStill turns that into a repeatable drill: one target at a time, played on your real bass, with immediate feedback and saved session stats.

  • Move through changesPractice the notes that connect chords instead of relying only on shapes.
  • Work in positionLimit drills to the neck area you are actually trying to improve.
  • Measure the responseSee whether recall is getting faster, cleaner, and more consistent.

Bass FAQ

Common questions

Can I use this for roots and chord tones?

Yes. Bass practice can focus on note names and harmonic targets like roots, thirds, fifths, and sevenths.

Is this a backing track app?

No. It is a focused recall drill. Use it next to songs, grooves, and backing tracks.

Is it just a tuner?

No. PitchStill asks for a target first, then checks whether you can produce it on bass.

Does it work with a real bass?

Yes. The app listens through your phone microphone during active sessions.
